# Drill inventory

Every drill the Coaches Corner practice ideas can suggest, what each one was matched to on Ice Hockey Systems (icehockeysystems.com, the site the coaches use), and when it changed. Read this when you want to know where a drill came from or go back to an older one.

How the card picks: the skating and skills warm-up changes every Monday and goes round the six sets in order. The focus areas below it are picked from the team numbers (goals against up, scoring down, two players with most of the goals, penalties high, close games going badly), up to three at a time, with "Keep the basics sharp" as the fallback. The rules live in `docs/js/model/practice.js`, next to the drills.

Where the drills came from: all 30 were written by Claude for this site on Sept 24 2026 from common hockey coaching practice, not copied from any book or site. On Sept 29 2026 each one was matched to the closest Ice Hockey Systems page that opens without a membership. "IHS drill" is one of their drills with a rink diagram; "IHS video" is one of their skill how-to videos. Links were checked on Sept 29 2026.


## Focus area: Tighten up in our end

### Breakout under pressure

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Full ice (half-ice version given)
- How: Coach dumps the puck in. A D gets it with one forechecker chasing, wingers on the boards, center low for support. Go up ice on the first good pass.
- Half ice: Stop at the red line and dump the puck back in for the next group.
- IHS drill: [1 on 1 Retrievals](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/1-on-1-retrievals) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Same idea: a D gets the puck with a forechecker on him and has to make the first good pass.

### Low-zone 2-on-2

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Half ice
- How: Two attack, two defend, below the tops of the circles. Defenders stay between their player and the net and keep sticks in the passing lanes. 30-second shifts.
- IHS drill: [2 on 2 Box Out](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/2-on-2-box-out-2) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Two-part net-front battle drill, defenders working below the circles.


## Focus area: Finish our chances

### Rebounds and tips

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Half ice
- How: Coach shoots from the point. Two forwards at the net: one screens and tips, one pounces on rebounds. Rotate every three shots.
- IHS video: [Tips and Deflections](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/tips-and-deflections-0) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: A how-to video on tipping and deflecting at the net. No free IHS drill was a closer fit.

### Shoot in stride

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Half ice
- How: Carry in from the blue line and shoot without stopping to stickhandle. Pick a corner before you shoot. Both sides, both hands.
- IHS drill: [Half Ice Shoot In Stride](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/half-ice-shoot-stride) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Same drill: receive at the red line, shoot before the cone, feet moving.


## Focus area: Get more players scoring

### 2-on-1, the other guy shoots

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Full ice (half-ice version given)
- How: Rush 2-on-1. The player who did not start with the puck has to take the shot, so everyone gets reps finishing a pass.
- Half ice: Start the rush at the red line instead of the far end.
- IHS drill: [Swedish 2 vs 1 (Cross Ice)](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/swedish-2-vs-1-cross-ice) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: A cross-ice 2 on 1 that runs as a station, so every kid gets the finishing reps.

### Three-touch small-area game

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Half ice
- How: 3-on-3 in one zone. A goal only counts after all three players on the team touched the puck. Keeps the puck moving to everyone.
- IHS drill: [Gates of Buffalo Small Area Passing Game](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/gates-buffalo-small-area-game) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Half-ice passing game built on moving to open space and quick passes. Add the three-touch rule.


## Focus area: Cut the penalties

### Angling with the stick down

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Half ice
- How: Checker steers the puck carrier to the boards with body position, stick on the ice. A hook, slash or high stick means the puck goes back to the carrier.
- IHS drill: [1 on 1 Angling Drill](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/1-1-angling-drill-10) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: 1 on 1 angling race with the same coaching point: steer with body position.

### Scrimmage with a real box

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Half ice
- How: Any small-area game. A stick penalty sends the player to a 30-second box and the team plays short. Kids feel what a penalty costs.
- IHS drill: [Picket Fences Game (2 vs. 2 or 3 vs. 3)](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/picket-fences) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: A 2 vs 2 or 3 vs 3 small-area game to run with the 30-second box rule.


## Focus area: Win the close ones

### Last-minute game

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Full ice (half-ice version given)
- How: Scrimmage with a set score and clock: down one with a minute left, then up one and protect it. Switch sides each round.
- Half ice: Play it 3-on-3 in one zone with the same score and clock.
- IHS: none
- Why this match: No match. IHS has no set-score, set-clock scrimmage. Run it as written.

### Next goal wins

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Half ice
- How: Continuous 3-on-3 across the ice, next goal wins, quick changes. Builds the habit of pushing for the winner instead of settling.
- IHS drill: [Continuous In Zone 3 vs. 3 Game](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/continuous-zone-3-vs-3) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Continuous 3 vs 3 in one zone with quick changes.


## Focus area: Keep the basics sharp

### Tempo passing

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Half ice
- How: Pass-and-follow triangles at full speed, tape to tape. Add a second puck once they have it.
- IHS drill: [Rondo Circle Passing Progression](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/rondo-circle-passing-progression) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Rondo circle passing progression, meant as a warm-up, half ice.

### Edges with a puck

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Half ice
- How: Tight turns, crossovers and stops around cones, head up, puck on the stick the whole way.
- IHS drill: [6 Cone Skills Series](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/the-6-cone-series) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Six cones, turns and crossovers with the puck, half ice.


## Warm-up set: Edges

### Edge circles

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Glide around a faceoff circle on one foot, inside edge, then outside edge. Both feet, both directions. Knees bent, chest up.
- IHS video: [Using Edges: The Basics](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/edges) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Their basics video on inside and outside edges.

### Figure 8s

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Figure 8s around two cones without stopping. Stay low and lean into the turns.
- IHS drill: [Figure 8 Edge Series](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/figure-8-edge-series) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Figure 8 edge series, the same drill with their progressions.

### Heads-up stickhandling

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Puck skills, half ice
- How: Stickhandle through a line of cones. Coach holds up fingers at the end and each player calls out the number, so eyes stay up.
- IHS drill: [Heads Up Stickhandling Station](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/heads-stickhandling-station) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Cone stickhandling station with the eyes-up coaching point.


## Warm-up set: Crossovers and turns

### Circle crossovers

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Crossovers around the faceoff circles, both directions. Push hard with the leg that goes under, not just the one that steps over.
- IHS video: [Forward Cross Overs](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/forward-cross-overs) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Their forward crossovers how-to video.

### Turn on the whistle

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Skate forward. On the whistle, turn to backward without slowing down. Next whistle, back to forward. Turn both ways.
- IHS video: [How to Perform a Transition Turn](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/transition-turns) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Their forward-to-backward transition turn video.

### Crossovers with a puck

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Puck skills, half ice
- How: Crossovers around the circle carrying the puck. Forehand going one way, backhand going the other.
- IHS drill: [Circle Warm Up Series](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/circle-warm-up-series-1) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Circle warm-up series: skating and puck handling around the circles.


## Warm-up set: Stops and starts

### Blue line to blue line

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Sprint, stop at the blue line, sprint back. Face the same wall every time so both sides of the stop get work. First three strides short and quick.
- IHS video: [Hockey Stop: Tips On How To Do A Side Stop](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/hockey-stop) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Their side-stop how-to video. The drill itself is just lines and whistles.

### Tight turns at the dots

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Skate to each faceoff dot and turn tight around it, stick on the ice, then burst out of the turn.
- IHS video: [Jam Turns](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/jam-turns) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Jam turns is their name for tight turns.

### Stop and go with a puck

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Puck skills, half ice
- How: Same stops and starts, carrying a puck. Keep it on the stick through the stop and take it with you on the first stride.
- IHS drill: [Start & Stop Loose Puck Battle](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/start-stop-loose-puck-battle) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Two players stop and start racing for a loose puck.


## Warm-up set: Puck control

### Backward C-cuts

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Backward the length of half ice using C-cuts, one leg then the other. Butt down, back straight, stick on the ice.
- IHS video: [How to Skate Backwards (for hockey players)](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/how-to-skate-backwards) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Their backward skating how-to video, which starts with C-cuts.

### Toe drags through cones

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Puck skills, half ice
- How: Pull the puck across the body with the toe of the blade at each cone. Start slow, then add speed.
- IHS drill: [Toe Drag Pull Shot](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/toe-drag-pull-shot) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Toe drag into a pull shot. The cone version is the first step of it.

### Protect it on the boards

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Puck skills, half ice
- How: In pairs along the boards. One carries, the other leans in with light pressure. The carrier keeps their body between the checker and the puck for 15 seconds, then switch.
- IHS drill: [Puck Protection Skill Drill](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/puck-protection-skill-drill) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Alex Tanguay's puck protection drill, body between the checker and the puck.


## Warm-up set: Passing on the move

### Mohawk turns

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Open the hips heel to heel and glide sideways, then turn up ice. Both directions. This is how D walk the blue line.
- IHS video: [Mohawk Pivoting](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/mohawk-turns) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Their Mohawk pivoting video.

### Backward partner passing

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Puck skills, half ice
- How: Pairs, one skating forward, one backward, passing the length of half ice. Switch at the end.
- IHS drill: [Defensive Transition Passing](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/defensive-transition-passing) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Passing while skating backward, run as a station.

### Take it on the backhand

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Puck skills, half ice
- How: Receive a pass on the backhand and move it on in one smooth motion. Both sides of the ice so everyone gets both hands.
- IHS video: [Backhand Passing](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/backhand-passing) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Their backhand passing video.


## Warm-up set: Backward skating

### Backward crossovers

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Backward crossovers around the circles, both directions. Look up ice, not at your feet.
- IHS video: [How to Do Backwards Cross-Overs](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/backwards-cross-overs) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Their backward crossovers how-to video.

### Pivot and go

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Skating, half ice
- How: Skate backward. On the whistle, open up and pivot to forward and sprint to the boards. Pivot both ways.
- IHS drill: [Defensive Pivot Races](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/hockey-drills/defensive-pivot-races) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Pivot races for defense, half ice.

### Pivot and pass

- On the card since: Sept 24 2026
- Ice: Puck skills, half ice
- How: Skate backward with a puck, pivot to forward, and hit a partner with a pass on the tape.
- IHS video: [Backwards to Forwards Pivot](https://www.icehockeysystems.com/skill-development-videos/backwards-forwards-pivot) (added Sept 29 2026)
- Why this match: Their backward-to-forward pivot video; add the pass.
